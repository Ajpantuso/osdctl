Automates the ROSA HCP disaster restore procedures.

The restore type (full or partial) is automatically diagnosed by inspecting the
management cluster state:

  full          Diagnosed when the HCP namespace is missing or Terminating,
                the HostedCluster CR is missing or being deleted, or etcd pods
                are CrashLooping. Sets ManifestWork updateStrategy to CreateOnly,
                creates a Velero restore from backup, waits for completion, then
                restores original ManifestWork strategies.

  partial       Diagnosed when the HostedCluster exists and the control plane
                is functional. Creates a Velero restore from backup without
                modifying ManifestWorks.
